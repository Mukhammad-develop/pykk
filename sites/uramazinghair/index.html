THE STORY

About Ur Amazing Hair

Ur Amazing Hair sits at the crossroads of old-school barber craft and forward-thinking style. Owner Johnny Sins built this chair with one belief: a great haircut changes how you walk into a room.

The space draws from classic barbershop heritage — leather, steel, and conversation — while the techniques stay current. Every fade, taper, and texture is executed with the precision that only comes from genuine attention to detail.

We do not rush. We consult. We shape. We finish with a hot towel and a moment of calm before you step back out onto Wesley Avenue. Whether you need a sharp weekly maintenance cut or a complete restyle for a new chapter, the chair is ready.

Located in Britannia, we keep hours that work for working people: seven days a week, 9am to 6pm. Walk-ins welcome when space allows, but booking guarantees your slot.
