Our story

A local shop, done properly

We’re a local barbershop focused on great haircuts, clean fades and friendly service. Whether you’re after a sharp trim, a tidy beard or a full grooming session, you’ll find the same care in every chair.

We take pride in our work and make sure every customer leaves looking and feeling their best. There’s no rush and no hard sell — just a relaxed atmosphere, a good chat if you fancy one, and a cut you’ll be happy with.
