About

A local barbershop that takes pride in the cut

We’re a local barbershop focused on great haircuts, clean fades and friendly service. Whether you’re after a trim, a beard tidy-up or a full restyle, we take pride in our work and make sure every customer leaves looking and feeling their best.

You’ll find us at 12 High Street, Watford, open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 20:00. Walk-ins are always welcome, or you can book a slot online if you’d rather not wait. No fuss, just a quality cut in a relaxed atmosphere.

Run by Jane Smith. If you have a question before you visit, give us a call or send a message — we’re happy to help.
