About

A local shop, done properly

We’re a local barbershop focused on great haircuts, clean fades and friendly service. Whether you’re after a sharp fade, a tidy trim or a proper beard groom, you’ll get the same care and attention every time you sit in the chair.

We take pride in our work and make sure every customer leaves looking and feeling their best. There’s no fuss here — just a quality cut in a relaxed atmosphere.

You’ll find us at 12 High Street, Watford, Monday to Friday. Walk in, call ahead, or book a time online.
